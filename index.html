Our 5-Stage Manufacturing Journey

From curated fruit extracts to automated, sterile packaging at our Tagore Nagar plant in Jaipur.

01

Ingredient Selection & Quality Inspection

Carefully selecting fruit extracts and safe natural ingredients checked by food testing standards.

Premium Ingredients
02

Precision Homogenisation & Blending

High-shear blending tanks ensure smooth consistency, even pulp distribution, and rich flavour texture.

Micro-Blending
03

Pasteurisation & Thermal Sterilisation

Controlled thermal processing eliminates microbial risk while locking in natural freshness and fruit vitamins.

Food Safety
04

Sensory & Lab Quality Testing

Every batch is checked for Brix (sweetness), pH balance, colour accuracy, and sensory taste approval.

Batch Testing
05

Cleanroom Automated Bottling & Capping

Bottles are rinsed, filled, hermetically capped, and batch-coded in a sterile cleanroom without human touch.

Airtight Sealing
